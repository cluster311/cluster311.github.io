Principles

There are few and they are not negotiable. If a project contradicts them, we do not take it.

  • No vendor lock-in The client can leave whenever they want, with their code and their data. It is the rule that orders all the others.
  • Free software If you want it, what we build is released under a free license. If you need it closed to the world but open to your team for auditing, we do it that way. Either way you can read it, audit it, change it and share it. If you cannot see the code, it is not yours.
  • Open standards Formats and protocols anyone can implement. Data has to outlive the vendor, the system and us.
  • Simple beats complex Fewer parts, fewer layers, fewer things that break at three in the morning. If a solution needs a long explanation, we look for another one.
  • Interoperability No system lives alone. Everything we build exposes an API and consumes everyone else's.
  • Not afraid of AI We use it where it helps, with the model as a replaceable part. We do not sell it as magic and we do not hide it.
  • Fair to the client We charge for the work, not for the ransom. We prefer a client who stays because they want to over one who stays because they cannot leave.
