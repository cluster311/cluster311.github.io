Services

Everything we deliver is open to the client and well documented. If you want to continue with another team tomorrow, you can.

Custom free software development

Management systems, web platforms, integrations and internal tools. Python on the server, plain HTML when it is enough, PostgreSQL for the data. All under a free license, in a repository that belongs to you.

Open data and data portals

Data portals built on CKAN, the most widely used open data platform in the world. Cleaning, normalization, catalogs, custom extensions and APIs.

Interoperability and APIs

We connect systems that do not talk to each other: documented REST APIs, importers and exporters in standard formats (CSV, JSON, XML or whatever you need), sync with third-party systems. We always prefer the open standard over the vendor's format.

Applied artificial intelligence

We use MCP to build agents that answer questions or take actions on your data, safely.

Vendor exit

If your organization is tied to a system that does not respect it, we help you leave: data extraction, migration to free software and a plan so it does not happen again. No drama, and nothing gets switched off until the new thing works.

Technical arm for NGOs

We work with organizations that help others stop depending on technology companies stuck in old models. We bring the technical team; you bring the cause and the ground.
