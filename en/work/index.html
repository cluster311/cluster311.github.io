Open data portals of the Province of Cordoba

2025-2026. Government of the Province of Cordoba, Argentina.

Full migration of the Province's two data portals: the Open Government portal and the Statistics and Census portal. Both ran on a CKAN version that had gone a decade without updates, on government-owned servers. We moved them to CKAN 2.11 on AWS with a database-level migration, rebuilt the themes and reorganized hundreds of datasets and organizations according to the client's inventory. The deployment infrastructure and the database-level migrator were released as free software.

We used these two new free tools for both portals, giving each team (different ministries) a private repository to manage what differs between them. We also built two custom extensions to manage the look and feel of each portal.

datosgestionabierta.cba.gov.ar datosestadistica.cba.gov.ar infrastructure (free software) migrator (free software)

Open data portal of the National University of Cordoba

2024-2026. Universidad Nacional de Cordoba.

Built from scratch on CKAN 2.11: design, extensions, Docker deployment and handover to the university's team on their own GitLab with continuous integration. For this portal we wrote and published extensions anyone can use: Apache Superset integration, database queries from CKAN, error notifications and a harvester for the SIU university information system (which in the end we did not use).

Almost everything we built lives in a GitHub organization called UnCKAN (CKAN for universities), so that other institutions can reuse it in the future.

datosabiertos.unc.edu.ar extensions (free software)

Open Budget of the Province of Cordoba

2020-2026. For an investigative journalism network.

For years we extracted, receipt by receipt, the budget execution the Province publishes on its transparency portal in a barely usable form. On top of that we built from scratch a Python and PostgreSQL platform that manages more than six million payment receipts from 2016 to 2026 and more than half a million suppliers. It lets you search by supplier, tax id, program or budget line, see inflation-adjusted amounts, track payment delays and download the data as CSV. It is a closed portal, invitation only, for journalists and researchers.
