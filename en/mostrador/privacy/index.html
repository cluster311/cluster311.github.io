Mostrador privacy

Mostrador is the AI-assisted WhatsApp support service that Cluster311 runs for itself and for its clients. This page says what it stores, what for, and how to have it deleted. It is short because we keep little and sell nothing.

What we store

Your phone number, your WhatsApp profile name and the messages of the conversation: the text, and for audio or images, what the system understands from them. It is used to answer you, to let a person pick up the conversation when needed, and to improve the answers.

If you mention something that helps us serve you next time (your name, your town, what you were looking for) we note it in your profile. We do not ask for more than that, and we delete it if you ask.

Who processes it

Replies are written by an artificial intelligence model. To do that, the text of the conversation is sent to the provider of the model we use at the time, which may change and may be in any country. We do not control what that provider does with what it receives beyond its own policy. We do not use your conversations to train models.

It is seen by the team of the organization that serves you through that number, and by Cluster311 as operator of the system. Nobody else. We do not share or sell data, and there are no ads.

For how long, and how to delete it

Data is kept while the service is in use. You can ask to see or delete everything we hold about you by writing to cluster311@data99.com.ar; we do it and confirm. To stop receiving messages from a number served by Mostrador, reply BAJA (or STOP).

Who is responsible

Cluster 311 SAS, Cordoba, Argentina, under Argentine Personal Data Protection Law 25.326. The Agencia de Acceso a la Informacion Publica is the authority you can complain to. If anything on this page changes, the date below changes.
